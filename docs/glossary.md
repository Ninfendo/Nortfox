# Glossary

The terms that NortFox's slices pin. A word can mean one thing on the screen and another in the law: the screen's Bokför gives a voucher its number, but in the law's sense an entry is bokförd only once its period is locked for everyone ([plan, section 2](plan.md#names)). So each slice's issue starts with its terms, and they gather here.

Each term has its Swedish and English word, what the law means by it, and its name in the API (`/v1`) and the code.

## Bokföring

Pinned by slice 2a, the ledger core in PostgreSQL.

| Term (sv / en) | What the law means | Name in /v1 and the code | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verifikation / voucher | The record of one business event: when it was compiled, the event date, what it concerns, the amount, the counterparty (BFL 5 kap. 6-7 §). | `Voucher` | |
| Verifikationsrad / voucher row | One bokföringspost: kontering and amount (BFNAR 2013:2 punkt 2.3). | `VoucherRow` | |
| Bokför / book (the button) | Not the law's "bokförd". | `book` | Booking gives the voucher its number, in the same transaction. A booked voucher is one that has its number (decision 2). There is no draft in the database. |
| Bokförd / final (the law) | Durable: it can no longer be removed or changed. Until an active lock, entries are "preliminär bokföring" (BFNAR punkt 2.1 with kommentar). | final; locked for an actor | A voucher is final when its date is locked for every actor (decision 2). A lock for one user or one kind of actor stops only that actor. For the others, the voucher is still open. |
| Ändra / alter | A change in place. A computer ledger never corrects a bokförd entry in place (punkt 2.17). A preliminary entry may still change (kommentar till punkt 2.1). | `alter` | Allowed only while the voucher's date is not locked for the actor. |
| Stryk rad / struck row | Striking through, the method of a manual ledger (punkt 2.17 andra stycket). | `Removed` | A struck row stays on the voucher, with who struck it and when. It counts in no balance. |
| Ändringsverifikation / reversing voucher | A rättelsepost: a new posting with its own verifikation (punkt 2.18; BFL 5 kap. 5 §). | reversing voucher; links `reverses`, `reversedBy` | A new voucher with its own number, linked to the old one both ways. |
| Radera / delete | Nothing bokfört may be erased (punkt 2.1). A preliminary entry may still be deleted (kommentar till punkt 2.1). | delete | Only the last voucher of its series, and only while its date is not locked for the actor. Its number is given out again. |
| Borttagna verifikationer / record of deleted vouchers | None. | `DeletedVoucher` | Each deletion is its own entry. Nothing in it is changed or deleted. |
| Verifikationsserie / voucher series | Voucher numbers in an unbroken series (punkt 5.9). | `VoucherSeries`, `NextVoucherNumber` | Per fiscal year. |
| Void range | None. | `VoidRange` | A recorded skip in a series, made only by a restore (decisions 2 and 7). |
| Räkenskapsår / fiscal year | The business's accounting year: normally twelve months, one for all its business (BFL 3 kap. 1 §, 4 §). | `FinancialYear` | It has no open or closed state of its own. A whole year is locked with the period lock. |
| Redovisningsperiod / period | A calendar month or similar (punkt 1.8). | period | The monthly periods of a fiscal year. A lock is a date, not a period: a period is locked when its dates are. |
| Låsning av period / locked period | The law has no such term. A lock is the active step that makes preliminary entries durable (kommentar till punkt 2.1). Once a period's work is finished, no later event or rättelse goes into it (kommentar till punkt 2.3 b). | `LockedPeriod` | A locked-through date for each user, for Intern systemanvändare, for Externt via integration and for Alla användare, plus the automatic lock. |
| Automatlåsning av period / automatic lock | As above. Its intervals follow the booking deadlines of BFNAR 2013:2 punkt 3.6-3.8. | automatic lock | Locks by an interval after the end of a month, a quarter or the fiscal year. It applies to every actor. |
| Bokslutsserie / closing series | The law has no such series. Bokslutstransaktioner are booked when the löpande bokföring is closed (BFL 5 kap. 3 §; punkt 2.19). | closing series | The period lock does not apply to it. |
| Manuell kontering / manual | None. | `Manual` | A series open for vouchers made by hand. |
| Behandlingshistorik / processing history | BFL 5 kap. 11 §; punkt 9.16-9.17. It is räkenskapsinformation. | behandlingshistorik (module `audit`) | Written by triggers, with the actor, its kind and the channel of each change. |
| Skapad / created | The registreringsdatum (punkt 9.16). | `created` at and by | On every voucher and every row. |
| Ändrad / altered | None. | `altered` at and by | Set by every change. |
| Konto / account | The kontoplan's account. | `Account` | Per fiscal year. |
| Företag / company | A bokföringsskyldig business. It keeps one bookkeeping for all its business (BFL 4 kap. 4 §). | `Company`; operations `getCompany` and `updateCompany`; in the company lists, `CompanySummary` | Every data request works in exactly one company, which its credential names. Slice 2c adds its postal and visiting addresses and its contact details, as the established company resource names them: `address`, `zipCode`, `city`, `countryCode`, `visitAddress`, `visitZipCode`, `visitCity`, `visitCountryCode`, `phone` and `email`. getCompany gives them; the company lists (`listCompanies`, `listRecentCompanies`) give `CompanySummary`, the id, the name, the organisation number and the tenant id alone. |

Where the established API has no name for a term, the code name here is proposed: `book`, `alter`, the links `reverses` and `reversedBy`, `DeletedVoucher`, `VoidRange`, the automatic lock, `created` and `altered`. The owner may change them.

## Platform

Pinned by slice 2b, the API and MCP spine. [docs/api.md](api.md) gives the API's rules.

| Term (sv / en) | What the law means | Name in /v1 and the code | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organisationsnummer / organisation number | The company's number in the register. For a sole trader it is the owner's personal identity number, so it is personal data. | `organizationNumber` | Ten digits with a hyphen after the sixth. |
| (no screen word) / tenant id | None. | `databaseNumber` | The company's number for programs ([decision 8](decisions/0008-authentication.md)). A whole number from 1 to 2147483647, given when the company is made. It never changes, and is never given out again. |
| Användare / user | The law asks when each entry was registered (punkt 9.16), and who made each correction, and when (BFL 5 kap. 5 §). | actor, with its kind: `user`, `system` or `integration` | On the screen, Användare names every kind of actor, not only a person. So the code says actor, and user is one of its kinds. |
| (no screen word) / channel | None. | `channel`: `rest` or `mcp` | The client a change came through. The behandlingshistorik records it. Later slices add others. |
| Belopp / amount | The books are presented in one redovisningsvaluta: SEK, or EUR for some company forms (BFL 4 kap. 6 §). | `Money`: `{"amount": "1234.50", "currency": "SEK"}` | Never a JSON number on `/v1` ([decision 5](decisions/0005-money.md)). |
| Behörighet / permission | Instruktioner om behörighet are räkenskapsinformation (BFN's commentary to BFL 1 kap. 2 § första stycket 9 c). | `permission`, such as `company:read` | Declared once for each operation, in the registry. Slice 2c grants them. |
| (no screen word) / operation | None. | one entry in the registry in `packages/contract` | The REST route, the client method and the MCP tool come from it. |
| (no screen word) / idempotency key | None. | the `Idempotency-Key` header; the `idempotencyKey` argument on MCP | A write takes one. The same key and request give the first answer again. |

## Login

Pinned by slice 2c, login, roles, tokens and MCP OAuth, as each of its parts adds them. [docs/api.md](api.md#signing-in) gives the sign-in's rules, [The companies](api.md#the-companies) the rules of companies and memberships, and [The API tokens](api.md#the-api-tokens) the rules of tokens and scopes. `packages/contract/src/terms.test.ts` checks that each name below is the one `/v1` has, and that each list of values below is the one `/v1` has, in its order. Two names are the exceptions, since only the behandlingshistorik holds them and `/v1` reads none of it yet: `AuditEntry`, the server's own type, and the actor kind `integration` with its via. The rows of Revisor, Läs and Bokföring - Attest & Läs name one value each of `Program` and of `Role`, whose rows list every value.

| Term (sv / en) | What the law means | Name in /v1 and the code | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Användare / user | No role. The law asks who made a correction and when (BFL 5 kap. 5 §), and when each entry was registered (BFNAR 2013:2 punkt 9.16). | `User`: `id`, `name`, `email`, `language`; `Membership`: `id`, `companyId`, `userId`, `status`, `adminLevel`, `idleTimeout`, `workEmail`, `workPhone`, `roles`, `programs`, `programsAdministered`; `Member`: the same with the person's `name` | `User` is the person. Its `id` is the actor of the person's changes, of the kind `user` (Platform, above). A person stays the actor on every entry, also once the identity is deleted. `Membership` is the person in one company, Användare there. Its `id` is the member's id in the company, which the paths of `/v1/users` take as `memberId`, since an invited member has no person yet; its `userId` is the person's. Its `status` is `invited`, `active`, `inactive`, `removed` or `left`. A membership is never deleted, and `removed` and `left` are final. `workEmail` and `workPhone` are the person's work contacts in that company, on the screen Användaruppgifter: the person changes them with `updateMyMembership`, and a system administrator with `updateUser`. Every active and inactive member has a work e-mail, so neither takes it away. `roles`, `programs` and `programsAdministered` are the member's roles, program grants and administered programs there (below). `Member` is a member as the user administration gives it, `listUsers` and `getUser`, with the person's `name`, or an invited member's latest invitation's. |
| NortFox ID / login identity | None. | the person's login data: `User`, and `Me` for the person's own | One per person, for every company. Companies share nothing of it. NortFox's own name, where the established system's is a brand. |
| (no screen word) / session | None. | `Session`: `user`, `company` | One person in one browser. Its cookie is the session's credential. A session works in no company until `selectCompany` ("Byt företag") chooses one. Then it works in that one company, with the permissions the person had there when it chose it, less a right whose grant has ended since. |
| Personnummer / personal identity number | Personal data (GDPR). | `personalIdentityNumber` | Twelve digits with a hyphen, `YYYYMMDD-NNNN`. Added once, never changed, and unique across identities. A sign-in takes it in 10 or 12 digits ([docs/api.md](api.md#signing-in)). |
| Systemadministratör / system administrator | None. | `SysAdmin`: `owner` or `admin`; `adminLevel` on `Membership` and `CompanyChoice` | The plan's owner and admin roles are this level. The person who makes a company gets `owner`, and a member made system administrator later with `updateUser` gets `admin`. Both hold the same rights, the company's details, its users and their programs, and every API token of the company, and neither gives work rights by itself. A company always has an active system administrator. Not the database's owner role ([decision 9](decisions/0009-company-isolation-and-database-roles.md)). |
| Utloggning efter inaktivitet / idle logout | None. | `idleTimeout` on `Membership`: whole minutes, `15`, `30`, `60`, `120` or `480` | Per membership, the established screen's five choices, 120 minutes (2 timmar) by default. A session that works in the company ends after so long without a request. The person sets their own with `updateMyMembership`. A session that works in no company ends after 120 minutes. |
| (no screen word) / role | None. | `Role`: `owner`, `admin`, `bookkeeper`, `approver`, `viewer` or `auditor`; `roles` on `Membership` | The plan's six roles, as sets in this model. `owner` and `admin` are the system administrator's two levels. `bookkeeper` is the program Bokföring with Full behörighet. `approver`, `viewer` and `auditor` are the programs Bokföring - Attest & Läs, Läs and Revisor. A member may hold several. `getMe` gives them for the current membership. |
| Programadministratör / program administrator | None. | `ProgramAdmin`: `program`; `programsAdministered` on `Membership` | Set per program, by a system administrator with `updateUser`. Grants, narrows, moves and removes that program, its Rapporter and its Register/Övrigt rights, and sees only those programs, as a session took them when it chose the company. Cannot appoint any admin. |
| Program / program | None. | `Program`: `bookkeeping`, `approval-read`, `read` or `auditor`; `ProgramGrant`: `program`, `permissions`, `full`, `grantedAt`, `expiresAt`; `programs` on `Membership` | A module with its permission set, Bokföring (`bookkeeping`) for example. A member holds a program once, and may hold several. A grant lists the rights it holds, in the catalogue's order. A member holds the rights of every grant that has not ended, so a Register/Övrigt right that two programs give stays while either does. A Revisor grant ends by itself 30 days after it was granted (`expiresAt`), and gives nothing from then on. |
| Full behörighet / full permission | None. | `full` on `ProgramGrant` | The default when a program is granted: every right of the program's set but those switched on by hand only. A grant that holds them all is full, also with rights switched on by hand beside them. |
| Behörighet / permission | "Instruktioner om behörighet" are räkenskapsinformation (BFL 1 kap. 2 § första stycket 9 c, per BFN's vägledning), kept like the books (BFL 7 kap. 2 §). | `Permission`: `key`, `group`, `label`, `view`, `manualOnly`; the group's key, `PermissionGroupKey`: `vouchers`, `supplier-invoices`, `reports` or `register`; the whole catalogue, `PermissionCatalogue` | A right of the catalogue, by its key, such as `vouchers.view`, in its group: Verifikationer and Leverantörsfakturor, the Bokföring groups, whose first permission is the view right that every other needs (`view`), and Rapporter and Register/Övrigt, whose switches each stand alone. `manualOnly` marks a right that Full behörighet never holds. `listPermissions` gives the catalogue in its order, with each program's set. Beside them, the platform permissions in the registry, such as `company:read` (Platform, above). Every grant and withdrawal is kept in the behandlingshistorik. No permission, the system administrator's included, gets past the ledger's rules in the database. |
| Bokför (permission) / book (permission) | On the screen, Bokför gives a voucher its number. In the law's sense an entry is bokförd only once its period is locked for everyone (Bokföring, above). | the `key` `vouchers.book` | The permission books vouchers. It does not make them final: the period lock does, and Låsa period (`vouchers.lock-period`) is the permission behind it. |
| Inbjudan / invitation | None. | `Invitation`: `name`, `email`, `expiresAt`, `companyName`, `inviterName`; an accepted one, `InvitationAcceptance` | Lägg till användare and Skicka en ny inbjudan send one by e-mail, to the name and the address an invited member has. Its link, Acceptera inbjudan, works once, for 14 days, and a newer invitation of the member stops it. Skapa ett nytt NortFox ID makes a new identity with the invitation's address; Koppla ihop med befintligt NortFox ID links the signed-in one. Either way the member's work e-mail becomes the invitation's address. The behandlingshistorik keeps each invitation, with its name and address. |
| Revisor / auditor | The company's auditor (ABL 9 kap. 7 §) and other external reviewers (SFL 41 kap. 9-10 §§). | the program `auditor`; the role `auditor` | The plan's auditor role. Read only: Bokföring and its reports, Behandlingshistoriken included. Exportera filer and Sie-export are off until switched on. The grant ends 30 days after it was granted, with no action by anyone (open point 3). |
| Läs / read | None. | the program `read`; the role `viewer` | The plan's viewer role. Read only: the view rights and the reports of Bokföring. No end date. |
| Bokföring - Attest & Läs / approve and read | None. | the program `approval-read`; the role `approver` | The plan's approver role (open point 2). See and attest vouchers, see supplier invoices, and read reports. |
| Behandlingshistorik / processing history | BFL 5 kap. 11 §; punkt 9.16-9.17. It is räkenskapsinformation. | `AuditEntry`, the server's type of an entry that changes no company row | Such an entry is written in the company, beside the rows that 2a's triggers write (Bokföring, above). A sign-in is Program "Användare", Rubrik "Inloggning". No operation reads the behandlingshistorik yet, so `/v1` has no schema for it. |
| Integration / integration | None. | the actor kind `integration`, with its via: `via_kind`, `api-token` or `mcp-client`, and `via_id`, the token's or the client's id | The kind of a request with an API token or from an MCP client ([decision 2](decisions/0002-ledger-law-in-postgresql.md)). It acts as the token's person, and its rights stay that person's ([decision 8](decisions/0008-authentication.md)): what its scopes and the person's permissions in its company both allow. The period lock's row Externt via integration applies to it. A session's kind is its user. The behandlingshistorik records the person, the kind and the token or the client the change came through, and the database refuses an integration's write that names neither. |
| (no screen word) / API token | None. | `ApiToken`: `id`, `name`, `scopes`, `userId`, `createdAt`; a new one, `NewApiToken`: the same with `token` | A token a member makes for a program, bound to one company and to the member's membership there, with a name and scopes. Only a browser session makes one, with `createApiToken`, so a token never makes a token. The `token`, `nft_` and 43 characters, is shown once, and the server keeps only its hash. A program sends it as `Authorization: Bearer`, on `/v1` only. It acts as the person who made it (`userId`), an integration (above), and may do what its scopes and the person's permissions in its company both allow, read on every request. It works until it is revoked. While its member is deactivated it names no actor, and a removal, a leave or a disconnect revokes it. A member lists and revokes their own (`tokens:own`), a system administrator every one of the company (`tokens:all`). The behandlingshistorik keeps each token made and revoked, Rubrik "API-token skapad" and "API-token återkallad". |
| (no screen word) / scope | None. | `scope` in each operation's `x-nortfox`; `Scope`: `companyinformation`, `profile`, `companies`, `users`, `tokens`, `bookkeeping`, `settings`, `costcenter`, `project`, `currency`, `supplier`, `supplierinvoice`, `payment`, `connectfile`, `inbox`, `archive` or `deletevoucher`; `scopes` on `ApiToken` | What a token may be used for. Each operation that a token may call names one, beside its permission: `getCompany` takes `companyinformation` and `getMe` takes `profile`, as in the established API, and NortFox adds `companies`, `users` and `tokens` for the modules that the established API has none for. The list, `SCOPES` in `packages/contract`, also holds the established API's names that no operation names yet, which later slices attach their operations to. A name is case-sensitive, and each scope reads and writes. A token without the operation's scope is refused it with 403 `scope-missing`, also when its person holds the permission. A browser session is not limited by scopes. [docs/api.md](api.md#scopes) lists the scopes and their operations. |
