Roles, programs and the user administration, the third part of login. `docs/api.md` gives the rules, under The companies, The users, The invitations, and The roles and the programs.

- A member's work rights come from their program grants: Bokföring, Bokföring - Attest & Läs, Läs and Revisor, each with the rights of the permission catalogue it holds. A Revisor grant ends by itself 30 days after it was granted, with no action by anyone.
- `getMe` gives, in a company, the membership's `id`, its `roles` (the plan's six: `owner`, `admin`, `bookkeeper`, `approver`, `viewer` and `auditor`), its `programs` and its `programsAdministered`, and the permissions of the member's grants beside the member's own. `updateMyMembership` answers the membership with the same fields.
- A session takes the person's permissions when it chooses the company with `selectCompany`, and keeps them until it chooses a company again: a right taken from the member still works in it, and a right given since does not, until then. A right whose grant ends, such as a Revisor grant's, stops at its end, also in a session that chose the company before.
- A system administrator, the owner or an admin, holds `company:update`, `users:read`, `users:admin` and `programs:grant`; a program administrator `users:read` and `programs:grant`, for the programs they administer, which a session takes and keeps as it does the permissions.
- No permission gets past the ledger's rules in the database: a member who holds every permission, and a system administrator, get the same refusals as anyone, such as for a voucher dated in a locked period.
- `listUsers`, `getUser`, `updateUser` and `removeUser` run the company's users, by the member's id in `/v1/users/{memberId}`: Aktiv användare, the system administrator's level, the programs administered, the idle logout and the work contacts, and Ta bort användaren, never yourself. A company always keeps an active system administrator: removing, deactivating or demoting the last one gets 422 `last-system-administrator`.
- `assignProgram`, `removeProgram`, `moveProgram` and `updateUserRights` grant, take away, move and narrow the members' programs and their Rapporter and Register/Övrigt rights. A program administrator does it only for the programs they administer.
- `listPermissions` gives the permission catalogue: the roles, the programs with their sets, the groups and every permission, in order, with the manual-only marks.
- `updateCompany` changes the company's name, its postal and visiting addresses and its phone and e-mail; `getCompany` gives them. The organisation number never changes.
- The work e-mail address is required: `createCompany` gives the owner the person's own e-mail address as the work e-mail, `updateMyMembership` and `updateUser` refuse `null` for it, and an install's existing members get their identity's e-mail address once.
- `inviteUser`, Lägg till användare, invites a person by e-mail, with a name and an address, and a company's invitations take turns without keeping any other company's requests waiting; `resendInvitation`, Skicka en ny inbjudan, sends a new one and stops the old link; and `disconnectUserIdentity` takes the identity from a member and sends a new invitation, which a new or an existing identity may accept. An invitation's link, Acceptera inbjudan, works once, for 14 days. With no mail set up for the install, the three get 422 `mail-not-configured` and change nothing.
- `getInvitation` reads an invitation by its link, and `acceptInvitation` accepts it, with a new identity, Skapa ett nytt NortFox ID, which takes the invitation's address and its TOTP secret and signs in next, or with the signed-in identity, Koppla ihop med befintligt NortFox ID. The member's work e-mail becomes the invitation's address.
- `leaveCompany`, Koppla bort användaren, lets a person leave a company; only a new invitation lets them back. A repeat of the leave with its key gives its first answer again. The last active system administrator cannot leave, nor be disconnected.
- A second identity with an e-mail address gets 422 `email-taken`, and a person's second membership of one company 422 `already-member`, also when a race gets past the server's own checks.
- The install's mail goes through its SMTP server, set with `NORTFOX_SMTP_URL`, `NORTFOX_SMTP_PASSWORD_FILE` and `NORTFOX_MAIL_FROM` (`deploy/README.md`). A user name in the URL needs the password's file, and the password's file a user name. `NORTFOX_MAIL_FROM` is one mailbox: a value that a mail program reads as two addresses or as a group stops the server at start.
- An e-mail address that the API takes is one mailbox, mailed as it is written: a display name, a comment, a list or a group gets 400 `bad-format`, and so does a domain that a mail would reach under another name, such as one with a soft hyphen, a zero-width space or a fullwidth letter, an A-label (`xn--`), or one that ends in a number. The local part holds at most 64 characters and the address 254, also in NFC, the form the server stores. The API's description gives such an address the format `idn-email`, with a pattern that every regex dialect reads. A person's or a company's name stands on one line: a line break or another control character gets 400 `invalid-value`.
- Two rules of the owner's for an address: one that can become an identity's, or a work e-mail, holds no character whose case fold is not its lower case, such as ß, ς, ſ or a ligature, so `straße.example` and `strasse.example` never share an identity; and NortFox sends mail only to a local part in ASCII, so `inviteUser`'s address and a new work e-mail have one. `completeSetup` refuses a break of the first, and `inviteUser`, `updateUser` and `updateMyMembership` a break of either, with 400 `bad-format`; the database refuses the first too. The owner's work e-mail from `createCompany` keeps the person's own address, whose local part may be outside ASCII: `disconnectUserIdentity` then gets 422 `work-email-not-mailable` and changes nothing, until another work e-mail is set. An install that holds such an address stops at the migration until it is changed. The sign-in still takes the address in any letter case.
- The client exports `Program`, `ProgramGrant`, `ProgramAdmin`, `Role`, `Member`, `UserRights`, `RightKey`, `CompanySummary`, `Invitation`, `InvitationAcceptance`, `Permission`, `PermissionKey`, `PermissionGroup`, `PermissionGroupKey`, `ProgramDefinition`, `RoleDefinition` and `PermissionCatalogue`.
